## Act 1 — 「逆転の序章」

### 1.1 — Stadium (5 s)
Wide aerial shot of a night stadium under floodlights, packed stands, scoreboard reading "HHS 0 – 0 BARTLETT". Camera sweeps down toward the pitch. Crowd roar, drums.

### 1.2 — The bench (5 s)
Slow pan along the HHS bench. @Luca (#7) sits, jaw clenched, eyes locked on the field. A coach off-screen calls "Sanchez!". @Luca looks up sharply. @Tucker (#11) beside him slaps his shoulder and shouts "Luca, now!"

### 1.3 — Aura entrance (5 s)
@Luca stands and sprints onto the pitch, a blazing blue aura flaring around him. He stops, stares down the Bartlett defense in a low-angle close-up. Monologue (male voice, Japanese): 「俺に続け…この試合は必ず、俺が変えてみせる。」 The crowd noise fades into a heartbeat.

## Act 2 — 「勝利への突破」

### 2.1 — Battlefield (5 s)
Fast montage feel: the ball rings off the post, a Bartlett keeper makes a diving save, HHS players pressing. @Gracen (#8, captain armband) wins the ball in midfield and looks up.

### 2.2 — Tiger Rush (5 s)
@Gracen whips a cross toward the edge of the box. @Luca receives it, feints, spins and slices past a Bartlett defender; a ghostly tiger silhouette roars behind him ("Tiger Rush"). @Tucker shouts from the side: "Luca, keep going!"

### 2.3 — Near miss (5 s)
@Luca shoots; the keeper stretches, the ball skims the crossbar. Crowd screams. @Luca, already turning back: 「ここで決める！」 He dribbles forward again, trails of energy behind him.

## Act 3 — 「勝利の一撃」

### 3.1 — Extra time (5 s)
Close-up on the scoreboard: "EXTRA TIME 0 – 0". Sweat drips from @Luca's chin. @Gracen, deep in his own half, launches a long pass over the top.

### 3.2 — Meteor (5 s)
@Luca bursts onto the long ball like a meteor, streaks of light, defenders converging from both sides. Time slows; he sees the opening. 「この一撃で、すべてが決まる！」

### 3.3 — The winner (5 s)
@Luca unleashes a blazing strike into the top corner, the net bulges. Announcer (English): "Sanchez with the winner!" Stadium erupts, final whistle. @Tucker and @Gracen leap onto @Luca. Final frame: @Luca standing tall beneath the floodlights.
