# HHS vs Bartlett — Guion de animación (basado en `video_de_instagram.mp4`)

_Fuente: video de Instagram, 56 s, 720×720, 24 fps. Es un partido nocturno en el que el #7 de HHS marca y la grada invade el campo para celebrarlo. El guion sigue la misma estructura que el video, en 3 actos de 15 s (3 planos de 5 s cada uno) y un cierre con el logo, listo para MiniMax H3 (Ref2VA)._

## Lo que se ve en el video (referencia)
| Tiempo | Qué pasa |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Marcador iluminado y presión de Bartlett (blanco/rojo) en ataque |
| 2–4 s | **#7 (HHS, negro)** dispara a puerta y el balón entra; el portero de Bartlett queda vencido |
| 4–7 s | #7 sale corriendo, levanta el puño y cae de rodillas mientras la grada empieza a bajar al campo |
| 7–20 s | Invasión de campo: compañeros (#17, #18) y estudiantes se le echan encima; abrazos y saltos |
| 20–30 s | Jugador sin camiseta grita de alegría; caras a cámara bajo los focos |
| 30–40 s | Deportividad: jugadores de HHS abrazan a rivales de Bartlett (#11 blanco); abrazo del #16 |
| 40–52 s | La grada canta y aplaude alrededor de la portería; foto de grupo improvisada |
| 52–56 s | Cierre con el logo del Mustang plateado sobre fondo negro |

## Acto 1 — 「最後の一撃」 (El último disparo)

### 1.1 — Marcador (5 s)
`Close-up of a glowing orange LED scoreboard at night, tense tie score, then camera tilts down to the pitch where Bartlett players in white and red kits press forward under harsh floodlights. Low drumbeat, crowd murmur building.`

### 1.2 — El disparo (5 s)
`@Luca in a black HHS kit, number 7, receives the ball at the top of the box, plants his foot and strikes it with full power. Slow-motion impact, shockwave rings around the ball, a blue energy trail streaks toward goal. A referee in a neon green shirt watches in the background.`

### 1.3 — ¡Gol! (5 s)
`Reverse angle from behind the net: the ball rips into the netting, the Bartlett goalkeeper in white frozen mid-dive. Net ripples in slow motion, then time snaps back to full speed. Explosion of crowd noise.`

## Acto 2 — 「歓喜の嵐」 (Tormenta de alegría)

### 2.1 — Celebración (5 s)
`@Luca, number 7 in black, sprints away from goal with his fist raised to the sky, then drops to his knees and slides across the wet grass, sparks of light spraying behind him. Behind him, the stands erupt and students pour over the railing.`

### 2.2 — Invasión de campo (5 s)
`Wide shot: hundreds of students and HHS teammates in black (numbers 17 and 18 visible) flood onto the floodlit pitch and pile on top of @Luca. Confetti-like light particles, joyful screaming, phones held high.`

### 2.3 — Grito de guerra (5 s)
`Medium close-up: a curly-haired HHS player, shirtless, holds his jersey above his head and screams with joy under a blinding floodlight, lens flare, @Tucker shouting beside him. Monologue (male voice, Japanese): 「やったぞ…俺たちの勝利だ！」`

## Acto 3 — 「誇り」 (Orgullo)

### 3.1 — Deportividad (5 s)
`Quiet moment: crowd noise fades. @Gracen, captain armband, in black, embraces a dejected Bartlett player in white and red, number 11. Soft backlight, slow camera push-in, gentle piano note.`

### 3.2 — Hermandad (5 s)
`Close-up from behind: HHS number 16 in black wraps a teammate in a bear hug. Camera rises to reveal the whole team and student section gathered around the goalmouth, clapping and chanting in unison.`

### 3.3 — Logo (5 s)
`Everything dissolves into darkness. A chrome-silver mustang horse leaps out of the black background in a burst of light and freezes mid-gallop as the team emblem. Final drum hit, then silence.`
